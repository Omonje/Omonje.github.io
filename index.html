How I work

  • Code is for logic, not plumbing. Short chains stay in n8n. Anything that makes a real decision, or repeats across workflows, moves to code.
  • Every build ships with retries, error alerts and two handoff documents. One is technical, so another engineer can pick it up cold. The other is in plain language, for whoever runs it day to day.
  • Alerts fire when something changes, not every time a check runs, so the channel stays worth reading.
  • A person approves anything risky. Agents draft and sort. They do not send, delete or merge on their own.
